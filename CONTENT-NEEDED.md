## Required before launch

**1. Medicare disclaimer numbers.** The footer currently reads "we represent [number] organizations which
offer [number] products in your area." Both figures need filling in.

This is not optional wording. Medicare rules require third-party marketing materials to state how many
organizations and plans you represent. It has to be accurate before the site is public on the real domain.
